Questions

Range hood installation FAQ

More answers on our ventilation & air quality page.

Can you vent a hood on an inside wall?

Often, by routing ductwork through the ceiling or cabinets. We assess the route on site.

What humidity should my home be?

Generally around 30–50% depending on the season. We can recommend and install the right equipment to keep it in range.

Do I need an HRV?

Newer, tightly sealed homes often benefit from one. We can assess your home and explain the options.
