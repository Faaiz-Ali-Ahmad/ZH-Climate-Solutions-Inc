FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Can't find what you need? Call 647-614-4019 or email zhclimatesolutions@gmail.com.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on any page — choose your service, a preferred date and time window, and your details. Our team will contact you to confirm the appointment. For anything urgent, call 647-614-4019.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. For no heat, no cooling in extreme weather, major leaks, burst pipes or electrical hazards, call us any time at 647-614-4019.

Are estimates free?

Yes, estimates for new installations and replacements are free. We explain your options and pricing before any work begins.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. ZH Climate Solutions Inc. is licensed and insured, and our technicians are trained for the work they perform.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Brampton and serve the GTA and communities across Ontario — see the full list on our Service Areas page. Share your postal code and we will confirm availability.

Which brands do you work with?

We install and service equipment from leading brands including Trane, Carrier, Lennox, Gree, Midea, GE Appliances, RunTru, Excalibur, Bradford White, A.O. Smith, Rinnai, Navien, Noritz, Aprilaire, ecobee and Kohler — and we service most other makes and models too.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We provide HVAC, exhaust, plumbing, electrical and maintenance services for offices, restaurants, retail and multi-unit buildings.

Is my online booking confirmed right away?

Online bookings are requests. A member of our team will call or email to confirm the date and time with you.
